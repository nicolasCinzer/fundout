Welcome to Fundout. If you trade at a prop firm — FTMO, MyFundedFX, The5ers, or any other — you know the drill: pass the evaluation, get funded, stay funded.

What nobody tells you is that **most traders fail not because of bad setups, but because of math they're not tracking**.

<Callout type="tip">
  Fundout gives you a real-time view of the numbers that actually matter: drawdown velocity, max daily loss proximity, consistency score, and more.
</Callout>

## What we built

The core of Fundout is a trade journal with a twist: every entry feeds a live analytics dashboard that tracks your position against the prop firm's rules — not just your P&L.

### Rule-aware tracking

Different firms have different rules. Fundout adapts. Whether your firm uses a trailing drawdown, a static one, or a consistency rule, the dashboard reflects it.

### The math behind funding

Here's the thing about evaluations: you're not just trying to be profitable. You're trying to be profitable *within a constraint box*.

```js
max_daily_loss = account_size * 0.05   // typical 5% rule
drawdown_remaining = equity_high - current_equity
safe_zone = drawdown_remaining > max_daily_loss
```

When `safe_zone` is false, most traders make their worst decisions. Fundout shows you this number in real time, before it becomes a problem.
