A proprietary trading firm — prop firm for short — gives you access to their capital to trade in exchange for a split of your profits.

The catch: you have to prove you can trade within their rules first.

## The evaluation model

Most prop firms use a two-phase challenge:

1. **Phase 1** — Hit a profit target (usually 8–10%) while staying within daily and overall drawdown limits.
2. **Phase 2** — Prove consistency at a lower profit target (usually 5%).
3. **Funded** — Trade a live account. Profit splits typically range from 70% to 90%.

<Callout type="info">
  The evaluation is not a trading test. It's a risk management test. Your job is to hit the target without breaking the rules — and those are two very different objectives.
</Callout>

## The rules that trip traders up

Every firm has variations, but the core rules are:

| Rule | Typical Threshold |
|------|-------------------|
| Max daily loss | 4–5% of account |
| Max overall drawdown | 8–10% of account |
| Minimum trading days | 4–10 days |
| Consistency rule | No single day > 30–50% of total profit |

The consistency rule is the one most traders ignore — and the one that fails them after an otherwise successful run.
